# Working papers

gui2de has a working paper series hosted in the **DigitalGeorgetown** repository, managed for us by
the university library. It can be access at [this link](https://repository.digital.georgetown.edu/collections/61804ae5-2089-417b-b83d-97b86bdcb896).

Publishing a paper on DigitalGeorgetown creates a DOI number for it and makes it part of the public record. It enables digital preservation, and integrates with Google Search and Hoya Search. It does, also, make content easier to find by AI bots and agents.

Authorized gui2de staff can directly upload to DigitalGeorgetown. This [video](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zM24wAVCIlpL26yGqxX0sZSrIaa74s5x) explains how to do so. If you want to be authorized to upload content to DigitalGeorgetown directly, reach out to the Executive Director.
