# Remote data collection

Phone surveys differ enough from in-person work to need their own procedures — call scheduling,
consent, and much shorter modules.

Start from the [workplan](workplan.md) and prepare the [cases dataset](cases.md) that defines your
sample. Adapt your [survey questions](survey-writing.md) for phone delivery, then code the
[callsheet](callsheet.md) that manages call attempts inside the instrument. Enumerators are
[trained over Zoom](training.md), and [high frequency checks](hfc.md) and
[backchecks](backchecks.md) run as they would in the field.
