# Ethics & IRB

All gui2de studies involving human subjects must be reviewed and approved by the Georgetown IRB,
in addition to any local IRBs, before data collection begins.

Everyone on a study needs two things personally: [CITI training](citi.md) and an
[IRB account](irb-account.md). The study itself needs a [protocol](irb-study.md), and any later
change to it needs an [amendment](irb-amendments.md) — including adding new team members.
Separately, everyone listed on an IRB must keep their
[conflict of interest](coi.md) disclosure current.

Interns and RAs must be added to the IRB before they can be given access to any data folder.
