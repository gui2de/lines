# Research design

Most of our research problems fall into two categories:

- **Identification problems** — having a proper counterfactual, which allows us to isolate (identify) a particular effect. Usually solved by an appropriate design.
- **Inference problems** — generalising from an observed repeated sampling process to an underlying population truth. Usually solved by a larger sample size.

A layperson's primer on this is our [Making Effective Investments](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1YgdKT8-_aNSwblfAAXobhkY01iBeKEFoL9CG4v7cqGI/edit?usp=sharing) presentation for the IEFG Fellows (September 2025).

Useful resources:
