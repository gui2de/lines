# Data sharing agreements

Moving data between institutions — in either direction — needs a Data Use or Data Sharing
Agreement. These are handled by Georgetown's Office of Technology Commercialization (JORA), not
by us.

1. Ask JORA for the **latest version** of the form — incoming and outgoing data use different forms, and the templates change, so do not reuse an old copy.
2. Fill in the form and send it back to JORA.
3. JORA obtains any internal approvals needed — university information security, and the IRB where the study is pending or the data sensitive.
4. JORA drafts the agreement and sends it to the partner.

Start this early. It runs on the other institution's timetable as well as ours, and data cannot
move until it is signed.
