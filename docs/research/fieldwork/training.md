# Enumerator training

Training is the single biggest determinant of data quality. Budget more time for it than feels
necessary, and plan a recap day afterwards — sessions routinely run longer than expected.

## Before training

- Keep sessions to about **10 participants, 15 maximum** — more than that and you cannot involve everyone or track attendance.
- Share the agenda and materials in advance. A [sample training agenda](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WlauLmwBfrxpBU8nvEIxxssnjE471xt9/view?usp=sharing) is available for guidance.
- Prepare the survey in printable form as well as on tablets, and set up [equipment](equipment.md) beforehand.

## During training

- Go through the questionnaire **question by question**, clarifying intent rather than just wording.
- **Assign each enumerator a module to lead** beforehand. They read the questions aloud and guide the discussion; the trainer steps in to clarify. It keeps everyone engaged and surfaces misreadings early.
- Have enumerators work through the instrument on their own tablet, checking skip patterns offline.
- Run practice interviews in pairs before going near a respondent.

## Piloting

End training with a pilot. If you can find pilot respondents, use the second day for it, then leave
**a day between the pilot and the real start** so the instrument can be adjusted.

Otherwise start with a **soft launch** — a small number of real surveys, using the data to check the
instrument before scaling up. Time it for the end of the week, so changes can be made over the
weekend, or build in a one-day break for the team to deliberate.

## Training remotely

Phone-survey teams are usually trained over [Zoom](../../onboarding/workflow/zoom.md). An
[example training presentation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/18tEc3x9huMWtLsmJl3znX8dX3i6OptfI/view?usp=sharing)
is available.

### Running the calls

- **Audio calls are more reliable than video.** Video is nicer but suffers badly when connections are unstable.
- Ask enumerators to join **30 minutes early**, to troubleshoot and practise muting, chat and voice controls with each other.
- Encourage use of the **chat** for questions — it also tells you who is still following.
- Everyone stays **muted unless speaking**. The trainer can mute and unmute people who cannot manage it themselves.

### Materials

- Attach materials to the Zoom invite **and** share them on the WhatsApp group as a backup.
- Enumerators need to read the survey PDF on the same tablet they are using for Zoom, so **do not share your screen throughout** — project the presentation only while presenting. Anyone who prefers paper can print the materials and be reimbursed.

### Working through the instrument

Have enumerators log off Zoom and work through the survey on their own, then run a mock interview by
calling a colleague as respondent and submitting a full survey. Allow about **three hours including
lunch**, then reconvene for a debrief.
