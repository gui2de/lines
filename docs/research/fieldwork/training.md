# Enumerator training

Training is the single biggest determinant of data quality. Budget more time for it than feels
necessary, and plan a recap day afterwards.

## Before training

- Keep sessions to about **10 participants, 15 maximum** — more than that and you cannot involve everyone or track attendance.
- Share the agenda and materials in advance.
- Prepare the survey in printable form as well as on tablets, and set up [equipment](equipment.md) beforehand.

## During training

- Go through the questionnaire **question by question**, clarifying intent rather than just wording.
- Have enumerators work through the instrument on their own tablet, checking skip patterns offline.
- Run practice interviews in pairs before going near a respondent.

## Piloting

End training with a pilot. If you can find pilot respondents, use the last day for it, then
debrief and adjust the instrument. Otherwise start with a **soft launch** — a small number of real
surveys, using the data to check the instrument before scaling up.

For phone surveys, see [remote enumerator training](../remote/training.md), which covers running
these sessions over Zoom.
