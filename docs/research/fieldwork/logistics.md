
# Logistics

Fieldwork trips often involve coordinating travel for multiple team members, working with local partners, and managing expenses across different grants. Start planning early, especially when the trip involves piloting, enumerator training, or data collection.

## Planning a fieldwork trip

-  **Confirm the fieldwork schedule** - Check with local partners about the planned activities, locations, and dates 
-  **Decide where to stay** - Accommodation may already be arranged or recommended by local partners. Otherwise, look for hotels with convenient access to field sites, training locations, and partner offices
-  **Coordinate with other travelers** - If several team members are traveling, it usually makes sense to stay at the same hotel and take the same flights, unless there are specific preferences or constraints
-  **Confirm the funding source** - Check which grant will cover the trip and whether there are any restrictions on eligible travel expenses (e.g., US federal funders have a requirement to use US airlines if some are available; some grants have specific caps on airfare or hotel costs)
-  **Finalize the itinerary and bookings** - Once the arrangements are agreed upon, book travel following [Travel](https://gui2de.github.io/lines/operations/travel/).
