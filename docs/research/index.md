# Research

The research lifecycle, from first design through publication.

Every project starts with [research design](research-design.md) and, where it involves human
subjects, [ethics approval](ethics/index.md) — which must be in place before any data is
collected. [Fundraising](fundraising/index.md) runs in parallel.

Once a project is live, [data management](data-management/index.md) governs how data is obtained, maintained, stored and
shared. [Primary data collection](data-collection/index.md) covers running a survey, in person or by
phone, and [fieldwork](fieldwork/index.md) covers the teams, kit and training behind it. Results move through
[data analysis](data-analysis/index.md) to [publication](publication/index.md).

**GU-PASS** is Georgetown University's research regulatory and compliance online system for submiting a few forms relevant in that process, including:   

- [Financial Conflict of Interest Disclosures](ethics/coi.md) (see: Ethics)  
- Main Campus [Internal Grant Proposals](fundraising/grants-internal.md) (see: Fundraising)  
- [External Grant Proposals](fundraising/grants-external.md) and Subaward Requests(see: Fundraising)  
- [Data Use/Sharing Agreements](data-management/dsa.md) (see: Data Management)  
- And other specialized forms, like Sabbatical Leave Requests.
