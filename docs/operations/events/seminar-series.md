# Seminar series

The development seminar series runs every semester on main campus, Thursdays in ICC 550. Speakers
are invited months ahead, for a whole semester at a time.

Roles: a **Seminar Director** owns the programme, a **Seminar Coordinator** runs the logistics, a
**Website Coordinator** keeps the public page current, and a **Finance Officer** handles payments.

## Three weeks out

- The Coordinator contacts the speaker to arrange travel and request a paper title and link. Put the seminar date in the subject line so the thread is easy to find later.
- gui2de typically covers return airfare or rail, one to three nights' hotel, taxis, and lunch and dinner with faculty.
- **Pay up front rather than reimburse** wherever possible. Use a procard and save receipts to the shared ProCard folder and the seminar Box folder.
- The on-campus conference centre offers a Provost rate, which needs a form signed by a gui2de officer. For off-campus hotels, ask the speaker whether they prefer Glover Park or downtown.
- If the speaker is **not a US citizen**, start the payment paperwork now — it takes longer.
- Confirm arrangements with the speaker and the Director once booked.

## Before seminar week

- Coordinator or Director passes the paper title and link to the Website Coordinator.
- Update the public seminar page and the gui2de Development Seminar calendar.

## Seminar week

- **Monday, ~10am** — the Coordinator sends the announcement from the gui2de mailbox to the general seminar list: Econ faculty, development PhD students, DC staff and RAs, affiliates, and McCourt students. **Send BCC**, so nobody can reply-all and no addresses are exposed.
- **Monday** — circulate the editable schedule to affiliates and PhD students asking for availability; ask for replies by Wednesday. Check the document is editable by people outside the organisation.
- **Wednesday** — Coordinator and Director close out the schedule and send the speaker a copy, with arrival instructions and who to find on the day.
- **Thursday** — final agenda to everyone on the schedule. The Coordinator may help with dinner reservations once a place is chosen.

## Paying the speaker

Afterwards the Coordinator works with the Finance Officer, copying them on all correspondence with
the speaker. Receipts are required either way.

- **US nationals** complete the university's online supplier form, selecting "non-reportable payee". Payment is by check or ACH.
- **International speakers** complete the Tax Department Request Form, and a Bank Wire Transfer Form if they want a transfer rather than a check. Wire transfer is faster and is the recommended route.

!!! warning "Bank details must not travel by email"

    Have the speaker hand over the wire form in person on the day, or send it by post. Never email it.
