# Money

Which route applies depends on what you are buying and how much it costs.

[Procurement](procurement.md) covers purchase orders, contracts and suppliers.
[Reimbursements](reimbursements.md) covers expenses you have already paid — file within 60 days of
the expense to avoid overcharges. The [procard](procard.md) covers small direct purchases, with
receipts filed within 30 days.
