# Procard

The procard covers small direct purchases.

- **Save every receipt to the shared ProCard folder** as you go.
- Receipts must be filed within **30 days** of the charge.
- For Amazon, Dell/Apple and Staples, use **Punchout** rather than buying directly.
- For flights and hotels, use **Concur**, the university travel agent — see [travel](../travel.md).

If you do not hold a procard yourself, ask your Research Manager who does.
