# Procard

The procard covers small direct purchases that can be made with a credit card. Per-transaction limit is $2,500.

- **Save every receipt to the shared [ProCard folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1XkN8DQ4UQMw0_8fYa5Z-rB9BwRt2oXt-?usp=sharing)** as you go.
- Add the transaction details in the [ProCard Charges](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VhrIMI5enGhqcEmlTluu2-_ivEBd1mvLxoSqz6JFax0/edit?usp=sharing) sheet if not self-explanatory from the receipt.
- Receipts must be filed within **30 days** of the charge.

If you do not hold a procard yourself, ask your Research Manager who does.
