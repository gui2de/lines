# Student RAs

## Hiring

- Use the [JD template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XkseVyKDGvNWSK-ZMtvLOduHS7yj_56y/edit) to hire student RAs — it includes language required by HR.
- Positions must be submitted for advertising (takes 2–3 days) and posted for five days.
- For FY27, the hiring date must follow the [FY27 New Hire Calendar](https://georgetown.app.box.com/s/spr25l5guqjv5kzhbvz4z7ko5s6e3hmv).
- FY27 rates are **$21** for undergraduates and **$26** for Master's students.
