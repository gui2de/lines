# Summer interns

gui2de runs a summer internship each year, with a training programme in the first weeks.

- Timelines, offer templates and past cohorts are in the [Summer Interns folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1UVwjSSZowg1YzVlRVhLFGapAGk8mnW7i) of the staff Shared Drive.
- Interns must be [added to the IRB](../../research/ethics/irb-account.md) before they can be given access to any data folder.
- Training material for the technical tools is in the students Shared Drive — see [technical tools](../../onboarding/tools/index.md).
