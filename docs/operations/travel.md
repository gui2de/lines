# Travel

- Book flights and hotels through **Concur**, the university travel agent.
- Where gui2de is hosting someone, it is better to **pay up front** than to reimburse them afterwards — it is faster for them and simpler for us.
- Charge what you can to a [procard](money/procard.md) and save the receipts to the shared ProCard folder.
- Anything you pay personally goes through [reimbursements](money/reimbursements.md), within 60 days.

Plan long trips and out-of-office periods with your colleagues, and tell partners ahead of time —
see [professionalism](../onboarding/workflow/professionalism.md).
