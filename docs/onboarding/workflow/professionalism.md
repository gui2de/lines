# Professionalism

What we expect of each other, whatever your role.

- **Meetings are preceded by an agenda**, sent by whoever called the meeting.
- **Meetings are followed by minutes**, sent within 48 hours — short, to the point, with clear follow-ups assigned to named individuals.
- **Emails are addressed within 24 hours**, either with a direct response or with a note saying when you will respond properly.
- **Workshops, field trips and conferences are followed by an internal report**, shared with the team within the week. If several people attended, coordinate on a single report.
- **Long travel and out-of-office periods are planned with colleagues** and communicated to partners ahead of time.
