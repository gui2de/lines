# For staff: intro to gui2de

gui2de is a joint initiative of the [McCourt School of Public Policy](https://sites.google.com/georgetown.edu/insidemccourt3/home), at 125 E Street NW in
Washington DC, and Georgetown's [Economics](https://econ.georgetown.edu/) department, on the main campus at 3700 O Street NW.

- Our main offices in DC are on the **5th floor at McCourt**; we also keep a presence on main campus, on the **3rd floor of Reiss**.
- We have an office in **Nairobi, Kenya**, and are opening legal presence in Tanzania and Rwanda.
- We operate projects across **East Africa and South Asia**.
- gui2de staff in DC meet for a **biweekly in-person working lunch** during the academic year.

## Seminars

- Weekly [seminars](https://gui2de.georgetown.edu/seminar-series/) with the Economics department, **Thursdays in ICC 550** during the academic year.
- Add the [seminar calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=mcujlsqh2v40khdpsa6qtqbgek%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FNew_York) to your Google calendar.
- *For faculty and staff only*: Seminars are preceded by an informal lunch with the speaker on **Reiss 4th floor**. Faculty, staff and fellows are welcome to join the lunch without RSVPing. They can also sign up in advance for a 1-1 slot with the speaker during the day; the sign-up sheet is circulated during the week by the Executive Director.
